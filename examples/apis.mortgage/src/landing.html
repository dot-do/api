What ships first, and who is liable for it.

You don’t need a license to know what the unregulated edge of a mortgage is. Extending credit, taking the Application, negotiating terms, deciding: those are Lender-Reserved, and they stay with the licensed operator who performs them. The edge is not reserved, and it ships first, from this property, in this order.

Why this door stands apart.

The license stays with the operator. The record stays with the software. Mortgage acts sit under their own federal and state regime, and an API in front of them moves none of them. apis.mortgage draws the line where the regime draws it: the software is the system of record — the loan file, the market record, the pipeline — and the licensed operator who runs on it brings the license, extends the credit, takes the Application, decides. You’ve seen mortgage APIs that turn out to be lead forms, and lead forms that turn out to be someone else’s license. This one holds the file and names whose license every regulated act runs on: the operator’s. A price posts on this page’s own rate card, and every row carries the status word it has earned. It never redirects into apis.finance. What is live here is live here, under this name, on the operator’s license and this software’s record.
