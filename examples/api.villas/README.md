The Lodging & Accommodation (NAICS 721) category's payable machine face at
the villa/short-stay sub-niche grain, instantiated from register row
`lodging` per the property-template spec
(studio `docs/plans/2026-08-23-property-template-spec.md`).

**api.villas is the row's proposed primary name** (ladder step 2 within what
is held; held pair with apis.villas). The row's caveat is on record, not

## Data honesty (source-route honesty — 100% labeled synthetic)

The row is **Axis-2 only by ruling** (avoid-class 5, "most crowded agent
lanes"). Its provisioned route — first-party booking/night-audit capture at
the rail — is an **unbuilt C-class rail** at wave zero and cannot be probed;
the only other lane, **public rate data, is the register-ruled avoid lane**
and was not touched. Consequence: **no Class-A source is honestly
reachable**, so every record on this sandbox is **labeled synthetic §5.2
seed** and `check.mjs` gates that no record improvises class-A status (no
`real: true`, no provenance blocks).

Synthetic records (`seed.mjs`) are **labeled example data** — deterministic
corpus, 952 demo prefixes, Example-name properties/operators/guests,
example.com emails, `example: true` on every record; folio and night-audit
arithmetic recomputes from the corpus (internally consistent by
construction). The sandbox is the real product over simulated data, never a
faked demo (live-demo ruling — seed = tenant #1, same handlers as product).

Pricing is a **402-shaped stub, labeled as such** in the `/pricing`
statement (`binding: false`): the OFFER boundary is real and typed, rate
rows price from zero or carry free quotas, and nothing bills. Metering /
money / receipt **seams** are emitted as structured logs tagged
`{substrate, projection, motion, operation, shape, pattern}` plus identity
class and referral source (spec §7.4 — seams only, no billing logic).
